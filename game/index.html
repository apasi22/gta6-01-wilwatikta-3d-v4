WILWATIKTA

3D open-world crime in a Javanese sunset city.
Sugeng Wibowo is back. Twelve missions, six districts, WPD chases, a police helicopter, and one last ride.

Drive the beachfront, steal the gold coupe, lose the helicopter.
Best played with a keyboard in desktop Chrome.

PAUSED

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows — drive & walk
E — enter / exit / steal car
Mouse click — shoot (drive-by too)
SPACE — handbrake / shoot
F — punch
R — reload
Q — switch weapon
T — radio station
G — garage (near Bengkel Bambang)
Tab — city map
P — pause   M — mute
H — horn   Shift — sprint

Follow the gold beacon to each objective. Lose wanted stars by breaking line of sight. The helicopter blocks wanted decay while it watches you.
